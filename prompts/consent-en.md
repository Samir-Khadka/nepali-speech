# Consent — Nepali Speech Dataset (version v1)

Please read this before recording.

## What is recorded?

You read aloud the Nepali sentences shown on screen, and we record your
voice. We record your voice only — nothing else.

## What happens to your recordings?

- Your recordings become part of an **open dataset**.
- The dataset is released under **CC0** — meaning anyone may use it for any
  purpose, including commercial purposes, without permission and without
  attribution.
- **No name, email address, or phone number is attached to your
  recordings.** We do not ask for or store those details.

## Optional information

You may optionally share: province, age range, gender. This information is
stored **separately** from your recordings and is not linked to your
identity. You do not have to share it.

## Your rights

- You may **stop at any time** — no reason is needed.
- You may **ask for your recordings to be removed**. To request removal,
  contact the project maintainers — the withdrawal channel is the GitHub
  repository's Issues page
  (https://github.com/Samir-Khadka/nepali-speech/issues).

## Consent

By recording, you agree to the above. Consent version: **v1**.

Thank you — your voice helps build open technology for the Nepali language.
