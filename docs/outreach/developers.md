## English version

Hello everyone! We are building the **Nepali Speech Dataset** — a community project creating open Nepali voice technology for everyone.

All we ask is this: read about **50 short Nepali sentences** aloud on your phone — it takes only **about 5 minutes**. No account needed, no cost.

Your voice helps build Nepali voice assistants and a phone-based assistant for farmers. Recordings are released under **CC0**, free for anyone to use — so anyone can build Nepali speech technology on this dataset. Please read the short consent text first — we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
